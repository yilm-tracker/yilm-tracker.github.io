Glossary
Week 8 is live. Every season number on this page counts Weeks 1–7 only (final). Week 8 shows in the Next line, the weekly results and the Matchups tab.
Weekly results tap the button under a team to open its week-by-week cards; they start closed.
Lean / Full Lean keeps each team to its seed, record, streak, standings range, games back and the PF and PA lines; Full adds the rest of the stat lines and the season chart.
Season chart one column per regular-season week. The bar is the team's score (green win, red loss) and the white tick is the opponent's score (gold frame when the opponent had the week's top score, burgundy for the lowest). RANK is where the team's score finished that week out of 12 (#1 gold, 2–3 green, 10–11 red, #12 red with an outline). OPP is the opponent's finish. Dashed columns are weeks still to play; the green dashed column is the live week.
PF and PA bars 12 segments, one per league rank: #1 fills all 12. Luck bar runs from a centre line: right is lucky, left is unlucky.
Games back games behind 1st, the last Bye spot (seed 2) and the last Play-Off spot (seed 6). Only the lines a team is behind are shown.
Movement arrow places gained or lost since the standings after Week 6. Hi / Lo / Avg highest, lowest and average standings position after each week.
Rank chips league rank for each stat: 1–3 green, 10–12 red, T = tied. Opp rk #1 = hardest schedule. Luck #1 = luckiest.
Opp rk average rank of your opponent's score among that week's 12 scores. All-play record if you played all 11 other teams every week.
All-play note all-play, xW and luck use the final matchup scores (the same scores that make up PF). The league site's own all-play figures come from schedule scores that miss some stat corrections, so they can differ by a game or three.
Luck actual wins minus expected wins (xW = all-play win % × games played).
Tot Eff points scored as a share of the best possible lineup each week (Max PF, using the whole roster incl. bench, with the league's QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, SUPER_FLEX slots). /wk = points per week left on the bench.
Dropped pts per wk each time a bench player should have started, the gap to the starter he replaces is credited to the bench player's position.
Could have Won: losses you would have won with your best lineup. Lost: wins you would have lost against your opponent's best lineup.
Median view adds a win each week for a top-6 score (out of 12) and a loss otherwise. Ties broken by PF. The league itself plays head-to-head only.
